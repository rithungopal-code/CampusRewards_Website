Know if a promotion paid off

Most owners can’t answer “Did that promotion actually make me money?” CampusRewards can.

How we measure it

Offers are randomized across comparable time windows, so some windows act as a control. A Bayesian model compares them to estimate the sales the platform actually caused.

What you get

A short, plain-language weekly report: students reached, redemptions, spend, and new versus returning customers, plus an honest estimate of extra sales and whether the promotion paid for itself.
